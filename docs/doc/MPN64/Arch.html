    The architecture layer on 64-bit limbs #

    What depends on the machine words of 64-bit limbs, beyond the shared code of MPN:

    • Arch.UInt: the high words of 64-bit sums, differences and products, inline C with __uint128_t;
    • Arch.UIntArray: the instances limbArray64, wordArray64 and wordFolds64 of MPN's classes on UInt64Array, from which MPN derives the rest (toomOpsW, fftOpsW, winOpsW);
    • Arch.MulBasecase: the schoolbook product with two rows per pass (GMP's mpn_addmul_2);
    • Arch.SqrBasecase: the schoolbook square, each cross product once;
    • Arch.LinComb: the kernels of the linear combinations of Lehmer's step (linComb64).

    Each is a choice for the host's multiplier, tuned on one machine, and the place to look first on another target.
