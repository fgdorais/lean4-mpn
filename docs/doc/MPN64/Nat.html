    Natural numbers #

    MPN.Nat is main's GNat on arrays of 64-bit limbs: a natural number as a machine word below 2⁶⁴ and as trimmed limbs above, with every operation proved correct against Nat. The instance natLimbs64 of NatLimbs gives it the operations of MPN.Num, whose results it trims (norm), and limb-wise bitwise operations (main's WordArray.landA, lorA, xorA). Since MPN.Num leaves at least one limb, the trimmed form here is one limb or a nonzero top limb (isTrim). The operations guard the bounds that the specifications of MPN.Num need, and otherwise compute on Nat, which never happens.

    The square root above 2⁶⁴ is Zimmermann's recursion over the bridge bridge64 to ops64 (Bridge.sqrtZ), and sqrtRem is it on arrays. The greatest common divisor is main's GNat.gcd on the instance gcdLimbs64 of GcdLimbs, with Lehmer's step and half-gcd over the bridge (Bridge.lehmer, Bridge.hgcd); MPN.gcdWith and Num.gcd are it on arrays.
