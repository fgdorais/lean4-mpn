    Toom multiplication #

    mulToom cfg r a b writes the a.size + b.size limbs of a * b into the slice r and sqrToom cfg r a the 2 a.size limbs of a², by main's MPN.Toom.mulToom and MPN.Toom.sqrToom on toomOpsW, from toomBase64: Toom-8, Toom-6, Toom-4, Toom-4 by 3, Toom-3, Toom-3 by 2 and Toom-4 by 2 by the sizes and the shape, with interpolations at the natural points 0, 1, 2, …, ∞, and mulKaratsuba or sqrKaratsuba below the thresholds of cfg. They require a.size + b.size ≤ r.size, and leave r unchanged otherwise.
