    The operations of the Karatsuba and Toom methods #

    The instance of MPN.Toom.ToomOps on UInt64Array: the linear operations are main's generic kernels (MPN.Toom.Kernels), and so is the middle term of Karatsuba's method (MPN.Toom.Mid); the base cases are the kernels of this library on USlices. Each of these checks that the indices of its windows fit in a USize and that the kernel's conditions hold; otherwise it writes the value of its specification with LimbArray.writeNat, which never happens.
