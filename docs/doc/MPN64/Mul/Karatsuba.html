    Karatsuba multiplication #

    mulKaratsuba r a b writes the a.size + b.size limbs of a * b into the slice r, like mulBasecase, by main's MPN.Toom.mulKaratsuba on toomOpsW, from toomBase64: from cfg.mulKaratsuba limbs it splits both operands at k = ⌈a.size / 2⌉, computes the three products recursively, and adds the middle term in one pass (MPN.WordArray.midW). It requires 0 < b.size and a.size + b.size ≤ r.size, and leaves r unchanged otherwise.
