    Divide-and-conquer division #

    Num.divMod a b and Num.div a b are main's Div.divRemG and Div.divQG (MPN.Div.Driver) on the limbs of b up to its top nonzero one, past a divisor of one limb (divModBase): the schoolbook method below config.divDC limbs in b, and above main's base-generic divide-and-conquer division (NatOps.divNorm, the scheme of Burnikel and Ziegler and of GMP's mpn_dcpi1_div_qr) on ops64B, the instance of NatOps with the schoolbook division at the bottom, of the operands shifted left by the bits that set the top bit of b, which keeps each digit's estimate within two of the digit; the remainder is shifted back.
