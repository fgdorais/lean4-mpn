    Division by a reciprocal #

    Barrett's division with a reciprocal from Newton's iteration, like GMP's mpn_mu_div_qr, on MPN.Num. The remainders, which are known to be small, are computed modulo β^N - 1 with the wrapped product of the FFT (Num.mulModB), at about half the cost of a full product.

    source
    def MPN.W64.Num.foldB (x : ScalarArray.UInt64Array) (N : Nat) :
    ScalarArray.UInt64Array

    One fold modulo β^N - 1: the low N limbs plus the rest, since β^N ≡ 1.
