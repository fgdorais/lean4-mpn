    Schoolbook division #

    divRem divides by a divisor v of n ≥ 2 limbs by Knuth's Algorithm D, like GMP's schoolbook division, through main's MPN.Div.Basecase on UInt64Array (Schoolbook.divRemN): the dividend and the divisor normalized by a shift, then from the top down, in one array, one quotient limb per step, or two by one pass of two rows from config.div2Rows limbs in the divisor, the multiply-subtract passes by the in-place folds. From config.div32Recip limbs in the divisor, the estimates are GMP's 3-by-2 step with the precomputed reciprocal recip32, checked exactly. The remainder is shifted back into an array of its own, and the quotient stays in the array of the division.

    The estimate with a precomputed reciprocal #

    GMP's schoolbook division estimates each quotient limb without the hardware divider, by Möller and Granlund's 3-by-2 step (udiv_qr_3by2) with the reciprocal ⌊(β³ - 1) / (v₁ β + v₂)⌋ - β of the top two limbs of the normalized divisor (invert_pi1). main's qEstR checks the quotient and remainder of the step exactly, q (v₁ β + v₂) + r = w₂ β² + w₁ β + w₀ with r < v₁ β + v₂, by four products off the dependency chain; then q is the quotient of the top three limbs by the top two, which is never too small. Otherwise it takes qEst. So neither the reciprocal nor the error bound of the step needs a proof.

          def MPN.W64.divRemWith (t : USize) (a b : ScalarArray.UInt64Array.USlice) :
          ScalarArray.UInt64Array × ScalarArray.UInt64Array

          (a / b, a % b), the quotient in a.size - b.size + 1 limbs and the remainder in b.size limbs, like GMP's mpn_tdiv_qr. It requires 0 < b.size ≤ a.size and a nonzero top limb of b. A divisor of one limb uses divRem1; a longer one main's Schoolbook.divRemN, which normalizes it, like GMP: a and b are shifted left by the k bits that set the top bit of b, which keeps the corrections of the estimates rare, and the remainder is shifted back at the end.
