    Whole arrays as numbers #

    Each function of MPN.Num returns a new array whose value, UInt64Array.toNat, is the result of an operation on the values of its arguments: add, sub, mul, sqr, shl, shr, low, lt and divModBase, on top of the mpn functions. The arrays may have zero limbs on top. Algorithms with few allocations per call, like the square root and the divide-and-conquer division (MPN.Div.DC), are written with them and proved on Nat.
