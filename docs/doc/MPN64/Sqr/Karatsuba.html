    Karatsuba squaring #

    sqrKaratsuba r a writes the 2 a.size limbs of a * a into the slice r, like mulKaratsuba r a a but with three squares, by main's MPN.Toom.sqrKaratsuba. Below cfg.sqrKaratsuba limbs it is sqrBasecase. It requires 0 < a.size and 2 a.size ≤ r.size, and leaves r unchanged otherwise.
