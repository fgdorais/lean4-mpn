    Double-width word operations #

    The high-word operations of limb arithmetic on UInt32 and UInt64, each computing the high word of a double-width result:

    • addh c x y adds the carry out of x + y to c;
    • subh b x y adds the borrow out of x - y to b;
    • mulh x y is the high word of x * y;
    • div2 hi lo d is the quotient of the double word hi lo by d.

    The low words are the ordinary wrapping x + y, x - y and x * y, so a limb loop computes the low word and the carry, borrow or high word separately, without allocating a pair.

    The UInt32 operations compute in UInt64. The UInt64 operations are specified with Nat and implemented by inline C with __uint128_t, so their generated C only compiles where the C compiler has that type, for example, GCC 4.6 or later and Clang 3.3 or later.

    Each operation comes with a toNat_* theorem that states its value in Nat.
