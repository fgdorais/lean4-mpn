    Greatest common divisor #

    The parts of the greatest common divisor that depend on 64-bit limbs, for main's GNat.gcd (MPN.NatGcd, with the instance in MPN.Nat), ported from main:

    • the cofactors of Lehmer's step (lehmer64, for main's LehmerStep): two runs of Euclid's steps on the leading words of the two numbers (cofactors2, with lehmerSteps in Int64), or one run when those do not apply, give cofactors of about 62 bits, which are checked on words (wmatOf: entries below 2⁶², determinant ±1); LehmerStep applies them to the full numbers in one pass each (mulSubMul). Nothing about the steps on the words needs a proof: a matrix of determinant ±1 keeps the greatest common divisor.
    • Half-gcd (NatOps.hgcdReduceP, hgcd64), whose base case up to hgcdBase limbs is the same steps with their matrix kept up to date in one pass per entry (LehmerStep.smallSteps, mulAddMul).

      Cofactors

      The cofactors of two runs of Lehmer's steps, like GMP's hgcd2: the first on the leading 62 bits, applied to the leading 128 bits of both numbers, then the second on the leading 62 bits of those, and their product, with cofactors below 2⁶². Nothing is proved about them: applyCof checks them.
