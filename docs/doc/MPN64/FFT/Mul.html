    Products and squares #

    The products and squares on 64-bit limbs, by MPN's choice of an algorithm (MPN.Dispatch) on the slices: mul and sqr choose by the sizes with the thresholds of cfg, fftMul and fftSqr use the FFT with 2ᵏ pieces when its parameters suit the sizes. Each writes into the slice r when it holds the result, and leaves r unchanged otherwise.

    The parameters #

    source
    def MPN.W64.FFT.fftK (T : Nat) :
    Nat

    The number of pieces, 2ᵏ, for T limbs: the least k with 4ᵏ ≥ 10 T, 10 being the fftKFactor of Config. From a sweep of k = 4, …, 11 at 1024 to 65536 limbs per operand (Zen 3, 2026-10-23): the best K = 2ᵏ has K² / T between 8 and 32, and this choice is within 6% of the best at every size measured.

                def MPN.W64.mul (cfg : Config) (r a b : ScalarArray.UInt64Array.USlice) :
                ScalarArray.UInt64Array

                Writes the a.size + b.size limbs of a b into r, by Dispatch.mul, which chooses by the sizes. It requires a.size + b.size ≤ r.size, and leaves r unchanged otherwise.
