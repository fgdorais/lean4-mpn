    Decimal strings #

    main's decimal strings by chunks (MPN.DecChunks) with chunks of nineteen decimal digits, the largest power of ten below β (dec64): toDecimal divides by 10¹⁹ until nothing is left, and DecChunks.step reads nineteen digits at a time and adds them to the limbs, by main's WordArray.divRem1A and mulAddA (MPN.DecWord). Both take quadratic time; Num.toDecimal and Num.ofDecimal? switch to divide and conquer (NatOps.toDec, NatOps.fromDec) on long numbers.
