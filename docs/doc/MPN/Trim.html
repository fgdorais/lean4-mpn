    Trimming, numbers as limbs, and comparison #

    The representation-level operations of both widths, on any WordArray, with valAt as their specification:

    • trimTo k x pops the zero limbs on top of x while it has more than k limbs, in place when x is not shared; isTrimmedTo k x decides that it has nothing to pop. A width that keeps no limb for 0 trims to 0 limbs, and one that keeps one limb trims to 1.
    • writeHalves a o len v writes v % 2^(w len) into the len limbs of a from o on, by halves above 16 limbs, so that the operations on v stay linear in all; ofNatW n is the limbs of n, one limb for 0.
    • ltWin compares two windows of limbs from the top down, like GMP's mpn_cmp, with the limbs past the end of the shorter one zero; ltA compares two arrays, by ltW on machine indices.
