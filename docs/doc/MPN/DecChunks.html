    Decimal strings by chunks #

    Like GMP's mpn_get_str and mpn_set_str below their thresholds, both directions work in chunks of k decimal digits, the largest power of ten below the base of the limbs (DecOps):

    • toDecimal a divides by 10ᵏ until nothing is left, which gives the chunks from the least significant one up, then writes the top chunk as it is and each other one with exactly k digits. It equals Nat.repr of the value.
    • step, folded over the characters, reads k digits at a time into a chunk and then multiplies the limbs so far by 10ᵏ and adds the chunk; finish does the same with 10ʲ for a last chunk of j digits. It skips _ and equals Nat.ofDigitChars of the digits, the value that String.toNat? gives.

    Both take quadratic time; on long numbers toDecimalWith and ofDecimalWith switch to divide and conquer (NatOps.toDec, NatOps.fromDec), with the chunks as base cases. The state of reading is a structure of the instance's, whose fields are unboxed: a structure over the limbs would box the chunk at every character.
