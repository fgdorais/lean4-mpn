    The operations of the FFT over WordArray #

    The operations of MPN.FFT.FFTOps beyond zeros, for any WordArray, in place on the windows they write. addRange and zeroRange are the kernels addIntoK and zeroIntoK of the Toom methods. addCoefK adds a coefficient to a region from its limb off on, with addIntoK on the part of the region from there. reduceK writes the residue modulo 2^(w n) + 1 of a pointwise product P of 2 n + 2 limbs: P_lo - P_mid in one pass over the window, reading both halves of P, then P_hi and the borrow with incrAt, and normAt. Each checks its bounds; otherwise it writes the value of its specification with writeNat, which never happens.
