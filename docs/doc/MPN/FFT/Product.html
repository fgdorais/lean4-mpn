    Products by the FFT #

    Schönhage–Strassen multiplication of a by b on one flat array, for limbs of any width w: their pieces of m limbs are loaded into two blocks of K = 2ᵏ windows of n + 1 limbs, transformed modulo F = 2^(w n) + 1 with the root 2^σ, σ = 2 w n / K, multiplied pointwise and reduced modulo F, and transformed back. Divided by K, the windows are the coefficients of the product of the polynomials of the pieces, which are less than F when K 2^(2 w m) ≤ 2^(w n), so they are exact; their sum at 2^(w m) is a b.

    The transforms are those of MPN.FFT.Windows. The other in-place operations on the flat array, and on the array of the result, are those of the class FFTOps, and the pointwise products are an argument, so that each branch uses its own multiplication. The operands are ranges of arrays: (a, as, al) is the al limbs of a from limb as on.
