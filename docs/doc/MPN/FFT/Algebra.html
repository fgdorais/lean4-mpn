    The algebra of the transforms #

    The arithmetic of Schönhage–Strassen's transforms modulo F = 2ᴺ + 1 on Nat, for any N, so for limbs of any width: congruences (MEq), the reversal of the bits of an index (rev), the identities of the butterflies of the forward transform by decimation in frequency and of the inverse one by decimation in time, and the coefficients that the transforms give (fft_coef), also when the pieces of the operands wrap around, for products modulo x^K - 1 (fft_coef_cyc). The transforms themselves, on residues in windows of limbs, are in MPN.FFT.Windows.
