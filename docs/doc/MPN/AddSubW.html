    Addition and subtraction over WordArray #

    addW z zo a ao al b bo bl writes A + B into the al + 1 limbs of z from zo on, and subW z zo a ao al b bo bl writes A - B into its al limbs, for the windows A of al limbs of a from ao and B of bl ≤ al limbs of b from bo, like GMP's mpn_add and mpn_sub: one pass over the low bl limbs reading both windows (addNStep, subNStep), then one pass over the rest of A with the carry or the borrow (add1Step, sub1Step), and for addW the carry out into the top limb. Each checks its bounds; otherwise it writes the value of its specification with writeNat, which never happens.

    addN and subN are the sum and the difference of two windows in new limbs, for any sizes: addN puts the longer window first.
