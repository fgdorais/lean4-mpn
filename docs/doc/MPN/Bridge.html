    From GNat to NatOps and back #

    A width runs the base-generic algorithms of MPN.Generic on an instance NatOps α of its own, and its numbers are GNat A. A Bridge A α is that instance with the conversions: toO from the limbs of a number to α, and ofO from α back to a number, which trims. Over a bridge:

    • sqrtZ, the square root by Zimmermann's recursion (NatOps.sqrtRemAux, GMP's mpn_sqrtrem) on x 4ᶜ of 2 n limbs with β^(2n) ≤ 4 x 4ᶜ, shifted back by c bits, with the base case newtonBase: a width's own for short numbers, and Newton's iteration (GNat.sqrtNewton) above;
    • sqrtRem, any square root with its remainder;
    • hgcd, one reduction by half-gcd of the top third of the limbs (NatOps.hgcdReduceP);

    and lehmer, Lehmer's step (LehmerStep.lehmerStep) on the limbs of two numbers.

    The instance of NatOps of a width and its conversions from and to GNat A.

    • o : NatOps α

      The operations of the width.

    • w : Nat

      The bits of a limb.

    • B_eq : self.o.B = 2 ^ self.w
    • one_le_w : 1 ≤ self.w
    • toO : A → α

      The limbs of a number as an operand.

    • val_toO (a : A) : self.o.val (self.toO a) = NatLimbs.val a
    • ofO : α → GNat A

      A result as a number.

    • toNat_ofO (x : α) : (self.ofO x).toNat = self.o.val x
