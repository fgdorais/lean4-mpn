    Bits of windows of limbs #

    log2W a o n is Nat.log2 of the n limbs of a from o, from the top nonzero limb down, which for trimmed limbs is the top one; bitW f a b r n writes f of the limbs of a and b, zero past their ends (LinComb.limbU), into the first n limbs of r, in one in-place fold: with f the instance's land, lor or xor, the value is the bitwise operation on the values (valAt_bitW); and ltW a b n compares, from the top limb down.
