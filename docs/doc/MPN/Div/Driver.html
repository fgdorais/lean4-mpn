    Division by a divisor of several limbs, for limbs of any width #

    divRemG o base toα ofα c t t₂ a ao m b bo n divides the m limbs of a from ao by the n ≥ 2 limbs of b from bo, whose top limb is not zero, like GMP's mpn_tdiv_qr past its divisors of one or two limbs: a dividend of fewer limbs than the divisor is its own remainder; below c.divDC limbs in the divisor, Algorithm D (Schoolbook.divRemN, which normalizes); from there, the operands are normalized (Schoolbook.shlTop, shlFit), divided by blocks or by Barrett's method over the width's NatOps (Dispatch.divN, with base at the bottom, on the numbers toα gives), and the remainder is shifted back (Schoolbook.shrArr). divQG is the quotient alone (Dispatch.divQN). Past the sizes of the fast paths the results come from the values, which never happens.
