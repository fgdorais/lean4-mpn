    Schoolbook division with normalization, for limbs of any width #

    divRemN t t₂ a ao m b bo n divides the m limbs of a from ao by the 2 ≤ n ≤ m limbs of b from bo, whose top limb is not zero, like GMP's mpn_tdiv_qr below its thresholds of division by blocks: a and b are shifted left by the s bits that set the top bit of b, a into a new array of m + 1 limbs, which divLoop2In divides in place, and b into a new array unless s = 0. The quotient and the remainder, shifted back, are copied out of the array of the division into new arrays of m + 1 - n and n limbs.

    divQN copies out the quotient alone, and modN copies nothing: it shifts the remainder back in place (rshDown) and drops the quotient limbs above it (popTo), so that the array of the division becomes the remainder.

    Normalization #

    shlTop and shlFit shift a dividend and a divisor left by the s < w bits that set the top bit of the divisor, into new arrays, the dividend with one more limb for the bits out of its top; shrNew shifts a remainder back. A shift by 0 is a copy. They serve this division and the division by blocks of MPN.Div.Driver.
