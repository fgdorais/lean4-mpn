    Products by a limb, for limbs of any width #

    mul1W, addMul1W and subMul1W are GMP's mpn_mul_1, mpn_addmul_1 and mpn_submul_1 on windows: the n limbs of a from ao times the limb x, written into, added to or subtracted from the limbs of r from ro, with the carry or borrow out. Each is one in-place fold over the window of r (foldThen), whose step reads a by uget and is a field of the instance: mulAdd2, the double limb y x + z + c, or subMulB, with its borrow in the instance's form. r and a are different arrays, since a second reference to r would copy it.

    foldUInPlace_spec1 is the value of such a fold for any step with a linear law.
