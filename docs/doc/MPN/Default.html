    MPN.Nat

    The default natural numbers #

    MPN.Nat is MPN.W64.Nat, the natural numbers on 64-bit limbs, with its functions ofNat, ofString? and sqrt; the rest of its operations are MPN's GNat and its instances. The natural numbers on 32-bit limbs are MPN.W32.Nat, from MPN32. This module is the library of the same name, apart from the shared root MPN, which both widths import.
