    Choosing a division and a square root #

    The division and the square root of both widths choose their algorithm by the sizes, with the thresholds of a DivConfig and a SqrtConfig. Each width normalizes its operands with its own shifts and calls:

    • divN, for a divisor of n limbs with its top bit set: Barrett's division with Newton's reciprocal of the top limbs of the divisor (NatOps.divMuT, as GMP's mpn_mu_div_qr) from divMu limbs, and below the divide-and-conquer division (NatOps.divNorm, the scheme of Burnikel and Ziegler and of GMP's mpn_dcpi1_div_qr), which goes down to the schoolbook division of the width below divDC limbs;
    • divQN, the quotient alone: by Barrett's division without the remainder of the last block (NatOps.divQMuT) from divQMu limbs, and otherwise that of divN;
    • sqrtBy, which takes the square root of a large number by the Zimmermann recursion of the width when the width says so, and by Newton's iteration (GNat.sqrtNewton) otherwise.

    Normalization only affects speed: divN_scaled gives the quotient and remainder of a / b from those of a f / b f.

    structure MPN.Dispatch.DivConfig :
    Type

    The thresholds of the choice of a division.

    • divDC : Nat

      The size of the divisor from which division computes its quotient in blocks of half the divisor, with recursive divisions (Burnikel and Ziegler), instead of the schoolbook method.

    • divMu : Nat

      The size of the divisor and of the quotient from which division uses Barrett's method with Newton's reciprocal, inside divide and conquer (so from divDC on).

    • divQMu : Nat

      The size of the divisor and of the quotient from which the quotient alone uses Barrett's method with Newton's reciprocal, which then skips the remainder of the last block, inside divide and conquer (so from divDC on).

            def MPN.Dispatch.divN {α : Type} (o : NatOps α) (c : DivConfig) (base : α → α → α × α) (U V : α) (n k : Nat) :
            α × α

            (U / V, U % V) for 0 < V < Bⁿ and U < V Bᵏ: by NatOps.divMuT when the divisor and the quotient both have divMu limbs, and by NatOps.divNorm with base below divDC limbs otherwise. Barrett's division takes the reciprocal of the whole divisor, which a short quotient does not repay.

            Equations
            • MPN.Dispatch.divN o c base U V n k = if c.divMu ≤ min n k then o.divMuT U V else o.divNorm base c.divDC U V n k
