    Joining windows of limbs #

    joinW k x xo xn y yo yn is the number x βᵏ + y mod βᵏ for the windows x of xn limbs from xo and y of yn limbs from yo, in an array z of k + xn zeros: the low min k yn limbs of y, then zeros, then the limbs of x, by two copies (copyW) into one array, instead of a shift, a cut and an addition, which take three.
