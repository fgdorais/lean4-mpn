    Arrays of words #

    WordArray A L gives arrays A of limbs L of w bits the reads and writes at USize indices of their in-place loops, and the word operations on limbs that carry loops use: sums and differences modulo 2ʷ with their carries and borrows, shifts and comparisons with zero, each with its value. WordFolds A L adds the in-place folds of lean4-scalar-arrays over one window (foldThen) and over one window while reading two others of the same array (fold2Then), which make the array exclusive once and then store without checks, and states that they are the plain loops of ugets and usets (foldUInPlaceThen, foldUInPlace2Then), which the proofs are about.

    Code written once over these classes, with @[expose, specialize], compiles in main32 and main64 to the loops on their own arrays of 32-bit and 64-bit limbs.

              In-place loops reading the same array #

              foldUInPlace2 n f arr rs xs ys hr hx hy j b replaces limbs rs + j to rs + n - 1 of arr while threading an accumulator, where f also receives limbs xs + j and ys + j of arr itself, read at the time of the step.
