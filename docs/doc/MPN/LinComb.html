    Linear combinations of two numbers by limbs #

    mulSubMul x y a b is a x - b y and its sign, and mulAddMul x y a b is a x + b y, for limbs a and b below 2ᶜ, in one pass over the limbs of x and y: the kernels of Lehmer's step, like GMP's mpn_hgcd_mul_matrix1_vector, and of the products of the matrices of half-gcd. Each writes a fresh array of max x.size y.size + 1 limbs by the in-place fold, reading x and y as zero past their ends.

    The steps of the passes are those of the instance (LinCombOps), each with its value: the carry of a x - b y is signed, and the instance carries it in a form of its choice, such as a word in two's complement or one biased to be a natural number, with its value sv, so that the chain from one limb to the next stays short.
