    The array operations of decimal strings, for limbs of any width #

    The operations that DecOps asks of a width's arrays, on any WordArray: isZeroA (by ltA), divRem1A, the quotient and the remainder by a limb (by divRem1W into new limbs), mod1A, the remainder alone (by mod1W, which writes no quotient), and mulAddA, a m + v (by mul1W into new limbs), the arrays trimmed to k limbs. Past the sizes of the fast paths they compute from the values, which never happens.
