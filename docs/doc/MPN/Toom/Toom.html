    The Toom methods #

    Splitting the operands into pieces of k limbs makes them the values at x = 2^(w k) of polynomials with the pieces as coefficients, and their product the value at x of the product polynomial. Its coefficients come from its values at a few points, each a product of about k limbs, which mulToom computes recursively. The points are 0, 1, 2, …, ∞, natural numbers, so that every intermediate value of the interpolation is a natural number (MPN.Toom.Interp).

    • Toom-3 (3 × 3 pieces, degree 4), Toom-3 by 2 (3 × 2, degree 3) and Toom-4 by 2 (4 × 2, degree 4, with the interpolation of Toom-3);
    • Toom-4 (4 × 4, degree 6) and Toom-4 by 3 (4 × 3, degree 5);
    • Toom-6 (6 × 6, degree 10) and Toom-8 (8 × 8, degree 14);
    • the squares of Toom-3, Toom-4, Toom-6 and Toom-8.

    Each method writes the product of the low pieces and the product of the top pieces into the window of the result, the values at the other points of each operand into windows of k + 1 limbs of one array (evalsW, one pass by ToomOps.evals3 for three pieces at three points), their products into windows of 2 k + 2 limbs of another (pointsW, pointsSqrW), and interpolates in place. mulToom cfg chooses by the sizes, and is mulKaratsuba below the thresholds of cfg.
