    The kernels of the Toom methods, for limbs of any width #

    The in-place operations of ToomOps on windows [o, o + len) of arrays of limbs, over WordArray and WordFolds: each checks that its windows fit in USize indices, runs one or two folds over the window written, and otherwise writes the value of its specification with LimbArray.writeNat, which never happens.

    • addIntoK and subIntoK add S to D and subtract it, a fold over the limbs of S (addC, or subMulB by one) and the carry or borrow through the rest of the window (FFT.incrAt, FFT.decrAt);
    • addMulIntoK and subMulIntoK add and subtract S v, by addMul1W and subMul1W and the carry or borrow;
    • zeroIntoK writes zeros;
    • evals3K, sub3LinK and sub3ChainK read or write three windows in one pass (WordFolds.fold3Then, WordFolds.fold3ChainThen): the values of three pieces at 1, 2 and 3, three windows minus S + vᵢ T, and a chain of three windows each minus v times the old value of the next.
