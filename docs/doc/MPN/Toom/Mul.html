    Products and squares by the Toom methods #

    mulToom cfg d o a ao al b bo bl writes the al + bl limbs of a b into d from limb o on: by Toom-8, Toom-6, Toom-4 or Toom-4 by 3 from cfg.mulToom8, cfg.mulToom6 and cfg.mulToom4 limbs in the smaller operand, by Toom-3, Toom-3 by 2 or Toom-4 by 2 from cfg.mulToom3, cfg.mulToom32 and cfg.mulToom42, by the shape of the operands, and by mulKaratsuba below or otherwise, with the products of the methods computed recursively. sqrToom does the same for squares.
