    The middle term of Karatsuba's method, for limbs of any width #

    With z₀ = a₀ b₀ in limbs [o, o + 2 k) of d and z₂ = a₁ b₁ in [o + 2 k, o + n), Karatsuba's method adds z₁ = t - z₀ - z₂ from limb o + k on, where t = (a₀ + a₁) (b₀ + b₁) has 2 k + 2 limbs. midW d o k n t does it in one pass over the window of L = min (n - k) (2 k + 2) limbs of t: limb i becomes d[o + k + i] + t[i] - z₀[i] - z₂[i] plus the carry, by the width's addDiffC, written into t, which nothing else holds (d cannot be written in place: the window overlaps the limbs of z₀ and z₂ that are still to be read). The window is then copied into d with copyIn, and the carry goes on above it with FFT.incrAt. Two subtractions and an addition took three passes over t.

    The carry from one limb to the next is between -2 and 1; the loop keeps it plus 2, a word between 0 and 3. Outside its conditions midW writes the value of its specification with writeNat, which never happens.
