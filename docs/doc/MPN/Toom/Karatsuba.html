    Karatsuba's method #

    mulKaratsuba cfg d o x xo xl y yo yl writes the xl + yl limbs of x y into d from limb o on, for limbs of any width w. For yl ≤ xl, it splits both operands at k = ⌈xl / 2⌉ into x = x₀ + 2^(w k) x₁ and y = y₀ + 2^(w k) y₁, and computes

    • z₀ = x₀ y₀ into the low 2 k limbs of the window,
    • z₂ = x₁ y₁ into the limbs above them,
    • (x₀ + x₁) (y₀ + y₁) into a new array, and adds z₁ = x₀ y₁ + x₁ y₀, which is that minus z₀ and z₂, to the window from limb k on (ToomOps.mid),

    all three products recursively. Below cfg.mulKaratsuba limbs, or when yl ≤ k, it is ToomOps.mulBase. sqrKaratsuba is the same with three squares, below cfg.sqrKaratsuba limbs ToomOps.sqrBase.
