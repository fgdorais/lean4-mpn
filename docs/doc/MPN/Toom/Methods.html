    The Toom methods #

    Generated by scripts/toom_methods.py; do not edit. Each method splits a into p pieces of k limbs and b into q, or squares a, writes the product of the low pieces and the product of the top pieces into the window of the result, keeps the values at 1, …, D - 1, D = p + q - 2, of each operand and their products or squares in windows of one array each (pointsW, pointsSqrW), and interpolates (interpDW). The products are those of a multiplier mul given as an argument, which mulToom makes recursive.
