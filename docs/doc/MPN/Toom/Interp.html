    The interpolations of the Toom methods #

    Generated by scripts/toom_interp.py; do not edit. Each interpolation of degree D takes c₀ and c_D, the products of the low pieces and of the top pieces, in their windows of the result, and the values w_j of the product at the points j = 1, …, D - 1 in the windows of L limbs at (j - 1) L of one array W, turns the w_j into the coefficients c_j in place, and adds them into the result. The points are natural numbers, so every intermediate value is a natural number: each subtraction takes away a part of the value it subtracts from, and each division is exact. The first stage of degrees 4 to 6 takes c₀ and c_D from three windows in one pass (sub3Lin), each run of three passes w_a - m w_b, w_b - m w_c, w_c - m w_e is one pass (sub3Chain), and the second stage of degree 4 is the three passes of toom3Stage.
