    Strings of decimal digits #

    isNatFast s is String.isNat s, which tells whether String.toNat? reads a number, by a fold over the characters (String.foldl) with a state of one byte. Lean's String.isNat runs a loop over a slice's iterator, which costs more per character than reading the digits.

    The state is 0 before a digit, at the start or after _, 1 after a digit and 2 once the string is rejected: a digit leads to 1, _ from 1 to 0, and anything else to 2. The string is accepted when the state ends at 1. Two predicates on lists of characters, G0 and G1, are what the states 0 and 1 accept, which the characterization String.isNat_iff matches.

        By bytes #

        isNatB runs the same automaton on the UTF-8 bytes of the string, String.getUTF8Byte, without decoding characters: a digit or _ is one byte, its value, and the first byte of any other character is not a digit nor _ (it is below 128 and not one of them, or 0xc0 or more), so that the automaton rejects at it, as at the character.
