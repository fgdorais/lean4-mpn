    High words of UInt32 #

    The high-word operations of limb arithmetic on UInt32, computing in UInt64, which both widths use: addh c x y adds the carry out of x + y to c, subh b x y adds the borrow out of x - y to b, mulh x y is the high word of x * y, and div2 hi lo d is the quotient of the double word hi lo by d. Each comes with a toNat_* theorem that states its value in Nat. The min of USizes, on their values, closes the file.

          def UInt32.div2 (hi lo d : UInt32) :
          UInt32

          The low word of the quotient of hi β + lo by d, where β = 2³², or 0 when d = 0. When hi < d the quotient fits in a word, and lo - q d is the remainder (toNat_div2_rem).
