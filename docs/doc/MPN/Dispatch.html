    Choosing a product #

    The products and squares of both widths, written into a window of an array like GMP's mpn_mul and mpn_sqr, choosing the algorithm by the sizes with the thresholds of a MulConfig:

    • mul puts the longer operand first; mulU splits a longer operand that is at least twice as long as a shorter one of at least mulUnbalanced limbs into pieces of the size of the shorter (mulChunked), unless the shorter one has mulChunkMax limbs and the longer one is in the range of the FFT for unbalanced operands; mulBal multiplies by the FFT (fftMulW) from mulFFT limbs in the larger operand, or mulFFTUnbal when they differ by 3 : 2 or more, when the smaller one has mulFFTMin limbs, and by the Toom methods otherwise (Toom.mulToom), which go down to Karatsuba and the base case of the width (ToomBase);
    • sqr squares by the FFT (fftSqrW) from sqrFFT limbs, and by the Toom methods otherwise.

    The FFT has 2ᵏ pieces for the least k with 4ᵏ ≥ c T, T the size of the product and c the factor fftKFactor of the width, and multiplies its pointwise products by the FFT itself from mulFFT and sqrFFT limbs, fftDepth levels deep, and by the Toom methods below (mulPD, sqrPD). Each function writes the limbs of its window only, and its window holds the product, whatever the thresholds.

    • mulFFT : Nat

      The size of the larger operand from which multiplication uses the FFT.

    • mulFFTUnbal : Nat

      The size of the larger operand from which multiplication uses the FFT when the operands differ by 3 : 2 or more, where the Toom methods are slower.

    • mulFFTMin : Nat

      The size of the smaller operand below which multiplication does not use the FFT, whose cost follows the total size, where that of schoolbook grows with the smaller operand.

    • mulUnbalanced : Nat

      The size of the smaller operand from which multiplication splits a larger operand that is at least twice as long into pieces of the size of the smaller one.

    • one_le_mulUnbalanced : 1 ≤ self.mulUnbalanced

      The pieces need at least one limb.

    • mulChunkMax : Nat

      The size of the smaller operand from which multiplication no longer splits the larger operand when that one is in the range of the FFT, since one FFT on the whole product is faster.

    • sqrFFT : Nat

      The size from which squaring uses the FFT.

    • fftKFactor : Nat

      The factor c of the number of pieces of the FFT: 2ᵏ for the least k with 4ᵏ ≥ c T.

                The pointwise products of an FFT with d levels of recursion below it, like GMP's mpn_mul_fft on its residues: the full product of two residues of n + 1 limbs by the FFT itself, from mulFFT limbs, with d - 1 levels below it, reduced afterwards like the Toom products; the Toom methods (mulP) at depth 0, below mulFFT limbs, or when the parameters do not suit the sizes. GMP multiplies its residues modulo 2ᴺ + 1 directly, by a negacyclic transform of half the size; the full product costs about twice that, so the threshold is mulFFT itself.
