    The greatest common divisor of natural numbers #

    GNat.gcd x y is Nat.gcd x.toNat y.toNat, by the same algorithms on both libraries:

    • Euclid's algorithm on words when both numbers are, and on pairs of words (gcd2, like GMP's mpn_gcd_22) when both fit in the instance's two words;
    • Lehmer's algorithm (gcdLehmer) above: the instance's step on the leading words of the two numbers, applied to the full numbers when it makes them smaller, and otherwise one step of Euclid's algorithm;
    • reductions by the instance's half-gcd while they make the smaller number smaller, from half limbs of it (gcdWith).

    The instance of GcdLimbs A gives the parts that depend on the width of the limbs: the high word of a product of words, the remainder of two words by one, the two words of a small number, and Lehmer's step and half-gcd with their laws, which are that they keep the greatest common divisor.

    class MPN.GcdLimbs (A : Type) [C : NatLimbs A] :
    Type

    The parts of the greatest common divisor of GNat A that depend on the limbs, with their laws: nothing more is needed of Lehmer's step and of half-gcd than that they keep the greatest common divisor; size and half only affect speed.

    • mulh : UInt64 → UInt64 → UInt64

      The high word of a b.

    • toNat_mulh (a b : UInt64) : (mulh A a b).toNat = a.toNat * b.toNat / 2 ^ 64
    • rem2 : UInt64 → UInt64 → UInt64 → UInt64

      (ah 2⁶⁴ + al) % bl.

    • toNat_rem2 (ah al bl : UInt64) : 0 < bl.toNat → (rem2 A ah al bl).toNat = (ah.toNat * 2 ^ 64 + al.toNat) % bl.toNat
    • fits2 : GNat A → Bool

      Whether gcd2 should take a number, as two words hi and lo.

    • hi : GNat A → UInt64
    • lo : GNat A → UInt64
    • val_two (x : GNat A) : fits2 x = true → (hi x).toNat * 2 ^ 64 + (lo x).toNat = x.toNat
    • lehmer : GNat A → GNat A → Option (GNat A × GNat A)

      Lehmer's step on x ≥ y: two numbers with the same greatest common divisor, or none.

    • gcd_lehmer (x y : GNat A) (p : GNat A × GNat A) : lehmer x y = some p → p.fst.toNat.gcd p.snd.toNat = x.toNat.gcd y.toNat
    • hgcd : GNat A → GNat A → GNat A × GNat A

      One reduction of x ≥ y by half-gcd.

    • gcd_hgcd (x y : GNat A) : (hgcd x y).fst.toNat.gcd (hgcd x y).snd.toNat = x.toNat.gcd y.toNat
    • size : GNat A → Nat

      The size of a number, in limbs.

    • half : Nat

      The size of the smaller number from which gcd reduces by half-gcd.
