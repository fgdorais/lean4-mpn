    Linear combinations of two numbers by words #

    The steps of main's LinComb.mulSubMul and LinComb.mulAddMul on 32-bit limbs, a x - b y and a x + b y for words a, b < 2³¹ in one pass over the limbs of x and y, and the instance linComb32: main's LehmerStep applies the cofactors of Lehmer's step to two numbers this way, and multiplies the matrices of half-gcd's base case.

    The value of a limb of a x - b y, x a - y b plus the carry, lies in (-2⁶³, 2⁶³). The step adds 2⁶³ - 2³¹ to make it a word, whose low half is the limb and whose high half is the next carry plus 2³¹, and carries that word, as subMulBiased does: each step is a sum and a difference of natural numbers, and the chain from one carry to the next is an addition and a shift. Fusing the two differences of a Lehmer step into one pass, which this file did, was 5–11% slower for gcd from 64 to 1024 limbs than two passes.
