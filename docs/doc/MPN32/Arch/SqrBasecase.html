    Schoolbook squaring #

    sqrBasecase a is the schoolbook square, after GMP's mpn_sqr_basecase. Squaring needs each product aᵢ aⱼ with i ≠ j only once, since it appears twice: a² = D + 2 S where D holds the squares aᵢ² on the diagonal and S is the sum of the products aᵢ aⱼ with j < i. That is about half the limb products of mulBasecase a a.

    S is built one row at a time: row i adds aᵢ * a[0...i] at limb i and stores the carry, with addMul1SetPre, which reads the first i limbs of a without a slice. After i rows, 2 S + D is the square of the first i limbs, since adding the limb aᵢ adds 2 βⁱ Aᵢ aᵢ + β²ⁱ aᵢ² to the square of Aᵢ. A last pass, sqrAddDiag, computes 2 S + D in place, like GMP's mpn_sqr_diag_addlsh1.

      Doubling and adding the diagonal #

      The last pass computes 2 S + D in place over S, like GMP's mpn_sqr_diag_addlsh1: limb k becomes 2 sₖ + dₖ plus the carry, where dₖ is the low or the high word of a[k / 2]².

            Squares by rows into wide accumulators #

            sqrRowsMul a adds the rows aᵢ a[i + 1...] into UInt64 accumulators at limb 2 i + 1 with addRow, as rowsMul does for products, which gives the products below the diagonal, S. The last pass computes 2 S + D and the carries in one: limb k is twice accumulator k, plus the limb k of the diagonal, plus the carry.

                The compiled square #

                sqrBasecaseFast squares by rows into wide accumulators (sqrRowsMul) from config.sqrRows limbs, and by Comba's method (csqMul) below, whose lower fixed cost made it up to 29% faster below 16 limbs on Zen 3. @[csimp] makes the compiler use it in place of sqrBasecase: both compute all 2 * a.size limbs of the square, and limbs of a given length are determined by their value.
