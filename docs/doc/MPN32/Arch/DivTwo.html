    Division by two limbs #

    divRem2 a d₁ d₀ divides a by the two-limb number d = d₁ β + d₀, like GMP's mpn_divrem_2: the divisor is normalized to a word V = d 2^s with its top bit set, a is shifted left by s bits (as the loop reads it), and one pass from the top limb down divides the remainder so far, a word below V, followed by the next limb, by V. The remainder shifted back is a % d.

    The pass uses div32Step, Möller and Granlund's step with a precomputed reciprocal, checked exactly and without branches; if a check failed, the division is redone with div2Step. That step estimates the quotient limb with qEstimate3, from the top three limbs of the window and both limbs of V, which is exact for a divisor of two limbs; div2Fix then decreases it while it is too large, which never happens but needs no proof that it doesn't: the estimate is never too small, so the loop stops exactly at the quotient. The comparison and the remainder use words only: q V = ph β + a₀ against W = R β + x.

          A step with a precomputed reciprocal #

          GMP's mpn_divrem_2 divides without the hardware divider, by Möller and Granlund's 3-by-2 step (udiv_qr_3by2): with the reciprocal v = ⌊(β³ - 1) / D⌋ - β of the normalized divisor, two products and a few corrections give the quotient limb and the remainder. With 32-bit limbs all of it fits in 64-bit words. div32Step checks its result exactly, q D + r = R β + x with q < β and r < D, from two more products off the dependency chain, so that neither the reciprocal nor the error bound of the step needs a proof. The step has no branches: a failed check gives the remainder D, which makes every later check fail too, and the division tests the last remainder once and divides again with div2Step if it is not below D, which never happens. Branches in the step cost more than the step itself: the compiler joins ifs between pairs and boxes their words, an allocation per limb. The loop runs in about 6.4 ns per limb where div2Step takes 18 (Zen 3).

              Normalizing on the fly #

              Limb k of a 2ˢ, for s < 32, is the high word of (a[k] β + a[k-1]) 2ˢ, from two limbs of a, so the loop can read the limbs of the normalized dividend from a as it needs them, without a shifted copy.

              ScalarArray.UInt32Array × UInt64

              a / d as limbs, untrimmed, and a % d as a word, for d = d₁ β + d₀ with d₁ ≠ 0, like GMP's mpn_divrem_2: the quotient limbs of a 2ˢ by the normalized D in a new array, from the top limb down, with div32Step on the limbs of a 2ˢ read from a with shlLimbU. If the last remainder is not below D, a check failed, which never happens, and the division is redone with div2Step on a shifted copy (divRem2Limbs), as it is for arrays too long for USize indices.
