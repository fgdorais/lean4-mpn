    Natural numbers as limbs #

    A natural number is stored as little-endian base 2³² limbs in a UInt32Array. The arithmetic works on slices of limbs, so it accepts trailing zero limbs, and its results are trimmed so that the most significant limb is nonzero.

    The specification is toNat, the value of the limbs of a slice by valAt, as in main's kernels. Trimmed arrays represent each natural number exactly once, and ofNat and toNat are inverse bijections between them and Nat.

                      Compiled conversions #

                      toNat and ofNat go one limb at a time, multiplying or dividing a growing Nat at each limb, which takes quadratic time. Their compiled twins split the limbs in halves and join or split the halves with shifts, which Nat does in linear time, so that the conversions take O(n log n). @[csimp] makes the compiler use them.
