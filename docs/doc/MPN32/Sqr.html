    Squaring #

    sqrLimbs computes all 2 * a.size limbs of a * a by choosing an algorithm from the size of a, like GMP's mpn_sqr, and sqr trims its result. Squaring is cheaper than multiplying: the schoolbook square computes each cross product once, and the Karatsuba and Toom squares have smaller squares instead of products.

    • below cfg.sqrBasecase limbs: mulBasecase a a, since the extra passes of the schoolbook square cost more than they save on small numbers;
    • below cfg.sqrKaratsuba limbs: the schoolbook sqrBasecase;
    • otherwise: MPN's Dispatch.sqr into a new array, the FFT from cfg.sqrFFT limbs and the Toom squares and Karatsuba below, from the thresholds cfg.sqrKaratsuba, cfg.sqrToom3, cfg.sqrToom4, cfg.sqrToom6 and cfg.sqrToom8.
