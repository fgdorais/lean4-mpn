    Division #

    divMod a b is (a / b, a % b), trimmed, with Nat's conventions: a / 0 = 0 and a % 0 = a. The divisor is first cut to its nonzero limbs. A divisor of one limb uses divRem1, and one of two divRem2. A longer one is normalized, like in GMP: both numbers are multiplied by a power of two f that sets the top bit of the divisor, which keeps the corrections of the quotient estimates few, and the remainder is divided by f again at the end: by shifts in divRemBasecase below config.divDC limbs in the divisor, and by mulRow before the division by blocks from there. Normalization only affects speed: the quotient of a f by b f is a / b and the remainder (a % b) f for any f ≠ 0.
