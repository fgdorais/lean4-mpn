    Schoolbook multiplication #

    mulBasecase a b is the schoolbook algorithm, after GMP's mpn_mul_basecase: one addMul1 of b per limb of a. It takes quadratic time and is the base case of the faster algorithms.

    source
    def MPN.W32.mulBasecase (a b : ScalarArray.UInt32Array.Slice) :
    ScalarArray.UInt32Array

    All a.size + b.size limbs of a * b, untrimmed, by the schoolbook algorithm. It runs a.size rows of b.size limbs each, so it is fastest with the shorter operand as a.

      The compiled product #

      mulBasecaseFast adds rows into wide accumulators (Mul.Rows) from config.mulRows limbs in all, and uses Comba's method (Mul.Comba) below, whose lower fixed cost (one array, no carry pass) made it up to 26% faster below 16 limbs on Zen 3. @[csimp] makes the compiler use it in place of mulBasecase: both compute all a.size + b.size limbs of the product, and limbs of a given length are determined by their value.

      source
      def MPN.W32.mulBasecaseWith (r w : Nat) (a b : ScalarArray.UInt32Array.Slice) :
      ScalarArray.UInt32Array

      mulBasecase, by rows into wide accumulators from r limbs in all, or by Comba's method, with whole products in its columns from w limbs, when the sizes allow it.
