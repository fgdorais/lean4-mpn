    Multiplication #

    mulLimbs computes all a.size + b.size limbs of a product by choosing an algorithm from the operand sizes, like GMP's mpn_mul, and mul trims its result. Below cfg.mulKaratsuba limbs in the shorter operand it multiplies here: no limbs give zero, one limb mulRow, and more the schoolbook mulBasecase with its rows over the shorter operand. From there it is MPN's choice (MPN.Dispatch.mul) into a new array: chunked products for a longer operand at least twice as long as a shorter one of cfg.mulUnbalanced limbs, the FFT from cfg.mulFFT limbs in the shorter operand, and the Toom methods and Karatsuba otherwise. The thresholds come from a Config, and mulLimbs uses the library's config.
