    Natural numbers #

    MPN.Nat is main's GNat on arrays of 32-bit limbs: a natural number as a machine word below 2⁶⁴ and as trimmed limbs above, with every operation proved correct against Nat. The instance natLimbs32 of NatLimbs gives it this library's operations on trimmed limbs, with a fast path for the division of up to four limbs by a word (divRem128). The square root by Zimmermann's recursion is defined here on MPN.Nat. The greatest common divisor is main's GNat.gcd on the instance gcdLimbs32 of GcdLimbs, with Lehmer's step and half-gcd's base case on 32-bit limbs from here.
