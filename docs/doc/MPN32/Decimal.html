    Decimal strings #

    main's decimal strings by chunks (MPN.DecChunks) with chunks of nine decimal digits, the largest power of ten below β (dec32): toDecimal divides by 10⁹ until nothing is left, and DecChunks.step reads nine digits at a time and adds them to the limbs, by main's WordArray.divRem1A and mulAddA (MPN.DecWord). Both take quadratic time.
