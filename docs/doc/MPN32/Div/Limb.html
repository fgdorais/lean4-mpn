    Division by a limb #

    divRem1 a d divides a by the limb d, like GMP's mpn_divrem_1, by main's WordArray.divRem1W: from the top limb down, each step by UInt32.div2Rem, one hardware division of 64 bits by 32, or from config.div1Recip limbs (config.div1Unnorm for an unnormalized d) by Möller and Granlund's step with a precomputed reciprocal, checked. By default it always takes the reciprocal, like GMP's mpn_divrem_1.

    a / d and a % d, like GMP's mpn_divrem_1, as a.size limbs and a limb: by WordArray.divRem1W, with the steps with the reciprocal from t limbs for a normalized d and from tu limbs for another, normalized by a shift; on Nat were the sizes not USizes, which never happens. It is exact when d ≠ 0.
